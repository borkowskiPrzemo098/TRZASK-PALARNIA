# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, published on GitHub Pages (portfolio constraint shared with sibling projects).

## Users

Coffee drinkers in Poland buying whole-bean or ground specialty coffee online for home brewing (drip, espresso, aeropress, moka). Mostly on the phone, often re-ordering a coffee they already know. Secondary audience: prospective clients of the portfolio author judging whether he can build a working shop.

## Product Purpose

TRZASK is a fictional small-batch coffee roastery (Łódź, Księży Młyn). The site is a mini e-shop: browse the current roasts, pick weight and grind, add to a cart kept in localStorage, review the order summary with shipping threshold, and place a demo order. Success = a visitor can go from landing to a filled order summary in under a minute on a phone, and the shop feels like a real roaster, not a template.

## Positioning

Roast date and roast profile are first-class: every bag shows when it was roasted and how dark, because freshness is what a local roaster can promise and a supermarket cannot. The name refers to "first crack", the audible moment in roasting.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Product list with filters (espresso / przelew / bezkofeinowa), product detail, weight (250 g / 1 kg) and grind choice.
- Cart in localStorage, quantity edit, remove, free shipping threshold, order summary + demo checkout (no payment, no data sent).
- All products, prices and roast dates are fictional demonstration data and must be labelled as a portfolio demo.

## Evidence on Hand

None real. No reviews, awards or customer counts may be invented as fact.

## Product Principles

1. Freshness is the proof: roast date visible everywhere.
2. Two taps to cart on a phone.
3. Honest demo: clearly a portfolio piece, checkout never pretends to charge.
